Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the game's settings and use Restore Purchases, if available in your version. Make sure you're signed in with the store account used for the original purchase.

Can I turn off daily reminders?

Yes. Change the reminder setting in the game or disable notifications for Snow Tracks in your device settings.

How can I change ad privacy settings?

Open the game's Settings and choose Privacy & ad choices where available. iOS tracking access can also be managed in system Settings.

How do I report a bug?

Describe your device model, OS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Avoid sending private or sensitive information.
