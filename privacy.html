Privacy policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Publication checklist: Replace [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME] and [CONTACT EMAIL] before using this policy in the App Store. Verify the final production build, regional consent flows, and App Store privacy disclosures.

Who operates Little Tracks?

Little Tracks is operated by Björn Ernst. Privacy questions and requests: babywearsit.help@gmail.com.

Game progress and preferences

Little Tracks stores puzzle progress, stars, inventory, gameplay statistics, and preferences on your device using local storage. This information is not uploaded to an account or game server by the game logic currently reviewed. Deleting the app typically removes this local information, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

Advertising and tracking

Little Tracks integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and its partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and—only where permitted—personalization. Actual processing depends on your region, device settings, consent, and the production SDK configuration. Google's practices are described in its Privacy Policy and partner guidance.

Your advertising choices

Where required, the app presents a consent form and provides a Privacy & ad choices option in its settings. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency may ask for tracking permission. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. Declining tracking does not necessarily prevent contextual ads or all SDK processing.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. These providers process payments and purchase records under their own policies. Little Tracks receives purchase entitlement/status information to grant purchased game items. Little Tracks does not directly receive your payment card details.

Optional notifications

If enabled, a daily local reminder is scheduled on your device. You can disable reminders in the app or operating-system settings. The reviewed reminder feature does not require a Little Tracks account.

Support messages

If you contact support, the information you provide (such as email address and message content) is used to respond to your inquiry and retained only as needed for support, lawful obligations, and dispute handling. Do not send sensitive personal information in support requests.

Retention, deletion and rights

Locally stored game data may be removed by deleting the app; platform backups may have separate retention rules. Contact babywearsit.help@gmail.com for applicable access, deletion, objection, or other privacy requests concerning information controlled by the developer. Data processed by Apple, Google, or their partners may also be subject to those companies' controls and policies.

Children

Little Tracks is currently designed for a general audience rather than specifically directed to children. The developer must review age rating, advertising configuration, and applicable children's privacy requirements before release.

Changes

We may update this policy as app features or service providers change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
